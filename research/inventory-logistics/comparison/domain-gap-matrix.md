# Domain Gap Matrix: Three-Way Comparative Analysis

| Capability / Area | Our Current ERP Status | MetaFresh Architecture | ERPNext Architecture | Gap Analysis | Required? | Priority | Architectural Reason |
|---|:---:|---|---|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| **Stock Ledger (Transaction History)** | **MISSING** (Calculates by scanning `erp_depot_item` across all time) | `M_Transaction` (Immutable row per physical move) | `Stock Ledger Entry` (Immutable ledger with running balance) | No chronological stock ledger. Severe O(N) performance bottleneck and audit risk. | **YES** | **P0** | Essential foundation for accurate stock, point-in-time valuation, and manufacturing WIP. |
| **Stock Balance Authority** | **FRAGILE** (`DepotItemService` + `erp_material_current_stock`) | `M_StorageOnHand` (Row-locked balance per locator) | `tabBin` (Row-locked actual, reserved, ordered qty) | Current cache `erp_material_current_stock` is updated asynchronously without row-level locks. | **YES** | **P0** | Prevents race conditions and lost updates during concurrent dispatches/receipts. |
| **Stock Soft-Reservation** | **COMPLETE** (`erp_stock_reservation`) | `M_StorageReservation` | `tabBin.reserved_qty` | Complete for Sales Orders via [`StockReservationService.java`](file:///e:/Codes/ERP/jshERP-master/ERPSystem-boot/src/main/java/com/jsh/erp/service/StockReservationService.java). Needs extension to Work Orders. | **PARTIAL** | **P1** | Existing service is robust; just needs multi-document support. |
| **Warehouse / Depot Master** | **COMPLETE** (`erp_depot`) | `M_Warehouse` | `Warehouse` | Complete. Holds site name, address, default flag, and tenant ID. | **STABLE** | **Stable** | Keep existing authority. |
| **Bin / Storage Locators** | **MISSING** (Only freeform `erp_material.position`) | `M_Locator` (X, Y, Z coordinates + priority) | `Warehouse` (Self-referential parent-child tree) | Cannot track physical bins (e.g. `RACK-1`, `RAW-STORES-BIN-02`). | **YES** | **P1** | Minimal 2-tier model (`Depot` $\rightarrow$ `Bin`) needed for organized warehouse and shop floor. |
| **Perpetual Inventory Valuation** | **PARTIAL** (Moving average calculated on the fly) | `M_Cost` + `Fact_Acct` | `valuation_rate` on `Stock Ledger Entry` $\rightarrow$ GL | Disconnected from outbound dispatch; COGS GL entry is not posted automatically. | **YES** | **P0** | Critical financial requirement for automated COGS and accurate balance sheets. |
| **Landed Cost Capitalization** | **COMPLETE** (`LandedCostVoucherService`) | `C_LandedCost` Allocation | `Landed Cost Voucher` | Complete for PO/GRN. Apportions freight/customs to GRN lines and posts to GL. | **STABLE** | **Stable** | Keep existing implementation. |
| **Pick & Pack Slip Management** | **MISSING** | `M_Package` / Picklists | `Pick List` DocType | Dispatches occur directly against SO without warehouse picking wave. | **OPTIONAL** | **P3** | Defer to later. Direct dispatch works fine for mid-market scale. |
| **Delivery Challan (Rule 55)** | **PARTIAL** (`DeliveryChallanService`) | Custom Shipment Format | `Delivery Note` | Complete for purchase returns; needs unification with sales dispatches. | **YES** | **P1** | Mandatory Indian GST requirement for transport without invoice. |
| **Bill of Materials (BOM Master)**| **MISSING** (Only ad-hoc Chinese assembly bills) | `PP_Product_BOM` | `BOM` (Multi-level with revisions) | No master BOM templates. Operators pick items manually every time. | **YES** | **P1** | Foundational prerequisite for discrete manufacturing. |
| **Multi-Level BOM Explosion** | **MISSING** | `PP_Product_BOMLine` (Recursive) | `BOM Item` (Exploded items table) | Cannot define Sub-Assemblies $\rightarrow$ Finished Goods hierarchy. | **YES** | **P1** | Essential for assemblies with intermediate sub-assemblies. |
| **Work Centers & Routing** | **MISSING** | `PP_Order_Node` / Routing | `Workstation` & `Operation` | No machines, hourly rates, labor capacity, or operation steps. | **DEFERRED** | **P2** | Keep light in Phase 1; simple flat overhead absorption first. |
| **Production Work Orders** | **MISSING** | `PP_Order` | `Work Order` | No formal production order authorizing factory work against demand. | **YES** | **P1** | Core manufacturing milestone. |
| **Material Issue to WIP** | **MISSING** | `M_InOut` (Internal issue to WIP) | `Stock Entry` (Material Transfer for Manufacture) | No WIP location. Raw materials consumed immediately upon bill save. | **YES** | **P1** | Essential to prevent phantom stock and enable realistic production cycles. |
| **Finished Goods Receipt & Scrap** | **PARTIAL** (Assembly bill handles flat sum) | `PP_Order_Cost` + FG Receipt | `Stock Entry` (Manufacture with scrap line) | Basic assembly adds parent and deducts children, but lacks scrap/yield %. | **YES** | **P1** | Must record scrap and allocate costs to finished goods. |
| **Subcontracting / Job Work (Rule 45)** | **MISSING** | `C_Order` (Subcontract) | `Subcontracting Order` & `Subcontracting Receipt` | No tracking of materials sent to job workers under GST Rule 45 challans. | **YES** | **P2** | Statutory requirement for Indian manufacturers outsourcing processes. |
| **Material Requirements Planning (MRP)** | **MISSING** | `PP_MRP` Engine | `Production Plan` (Gross-to-net demand explosion) | No automated PR/WO generation from open sales demand and low stock. | **DEFERRED** | **P2** | Must come AFTER BOM, Work Orders, and Stock Ledger are proven stable. |
