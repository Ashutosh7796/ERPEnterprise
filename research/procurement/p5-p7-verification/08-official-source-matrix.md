## 1. Master Statutory Authority Matrix

| Topic / Phase | Governing Law & Authority | Section / Rule / Circular | Key Mandatory Requirement | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P5: Inventory Recognition** | Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) / ICAI | **Ind AS 2 / AS 2** (Para 10-15) | Inventories recognized at lower of cost and NRV. Acquisition costs include non-recoverable taxes, inward freight, insurance. Storage/admin costs excluded. | **CONFIRMED** |
| **P5: Accrued Liability (GRNI)** | MCA / ICAI | **Ind AS 37 / Ind AS 1** | Obligation to pay arises upon receipt & acceptance of goods; recognized as accrued liability prior to final invoice. | **CONFIRMED** |
| **P5: Landed Cost Capitalization** | MCA / ICAI | **Ind AS 2** (Para 11) | Direct costs attributable to bringing inventory to its present location and condition must be added to inventory cost. | **CONFIRMED** |
| **P5: Rupee Rounding** | Central Board of Indirect Taxes & Customs (CBIC) | **CGST Act, 2017 Section 170** | Tax, interest, penalty, or amount payable rounded off to the nearest rupee (50 paise or more rounded up). | **CONFIRMED** |
| **P6: TDS on Purchase of Goods** | Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) / Income Tax Dept | **Income-tax Act, Section 194Q** | Buyer with turnover $> \text{₹10 Cr}$ in preceding FY must deduct 0.1% TDS on purchases from resident seller exceeding ₹50L in FY. Deducted at earlier of credit or payment. | **CONFIRMED** |
| **P6: TDS on Non-PAN / Non-Filers** | CBDT / Income Tax Dept | **Income-tax Act, Section 206AA & 206AB** | 5% higher rate for non-furnishing of PAN or specified non-filers. | **CONFIRMED** |
| **P6: 194Q Guidelines & Advance TDS** | CBDT | **CBDT Circular No. 13 of 2021** | TDS on invoice excludes GST component; TDS on advance applies to gross advance amount. Precedence over 206C(1H). | **CONFIRMED** |
| **P6: MSME Statutory Timelines** | Ministry of MSME | **MSMED Act, 2006 Section 15** | Payment within 45 days (with written agreement) or 15 days (without agreement) from acceptance/deemed acceptance. | **CONFIRMED** |
| **P6: MSME 43B(h) Disallowance** | CBDT / Finance Act, 2023 | **Income-tax Act, Section 43B(h)** | Sums unpaid to Micro/Small enterprises beyond MSMED Sec 15 limit disallowed in FY of purchase; deductible only in FY of payment. Medium enterprises & traders excluded. | **CONFIRMED** |
| **P6: MSME Penal Interest** | Ministry of MSME / CBDT | **MSMED Act, Section 16 & 23** | Compound interest at 3x RBI Bank Rate with monthly rests. Strictly non-deductible under Income-tax Act. | **CONFIRMED** |
| **P7: Statutory Credit/Debit Notes** | CBIC | **CGST Act, Section 34(1) & (3)** | Credit Note issued by **Supplier** upon goods return. Debit Note issued by **Supplier** for tax/value escalation. | **CONFIRMED** |
| **P7: Physical Goods Return Challan** | CBIC | **CGST Rules, 2017 Rule 55** | Delivery Challan generated by Buyer for transportation of returned goods back to vendor. | **CONFIRMED** |
| **P7: Input Tax Credit Reversal** | CBIC | **CGST Act, Section 34(2) & Rule 37A** | Recipient must reverse corresponding ITC in GSTR-3B Table 4(B)(2) upon return/credit note. | **CONFIRMED** |
| **P7: Blocked ITC Treatment** | CBIC | **CGST Act, Section 17(5)** | Ineligible goods tax capitalized into inventory asset cost; return adjusts asset value without touching input tax accounts. | **CONFIRMED** |
